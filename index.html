FAQ

Quick answers

What is the difference between SEO, GEO and AEO?

SEO earns rankings in Google's results. AEO structures content to win featured snippets, People Also Ask and voice answers. GEO optimizes your brand to be cited inside AI answers from tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. I work on all three because they reinforce each other.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Technical fixes can show movement within weeks, but competitive rankings and revenue impact usually build over 3 to 6 months. After the audit I give you a realistic timeline for your site and niche.

Do you work with Shopify and Wix sites?

Yes. Shopify and Wix SEO are core specialties, and I optimize within each platform's constraints so you don't need to migrate.

What do I get in the free mini-audit?

A short written review of your site's biggest technical and on-page opportunities, covering a few key pages, with clear next steps. It is a diagnostic, not a full strategy.
